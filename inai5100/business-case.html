The Business Case

Seven sections, written for one real person at one real organization. This is the draft of your Final Project proposal — what you write here you will carry into Week 13 and build on, so pick a process you actually want to work on.

What a business case is for

It is not a description of a project. It is a document that lets one specific person decide, and it succeeds when they can say yes or no without a meeting. That means it has to contain the things they would otherwise have to ask for — what it costs, what changes, what it does not fix, and what you would accept as evidence that it failed.

What most proposals do

Argue for the project

Benefits listed, risks hedged, no alternative considered. It reads as advocacy, so the reader's job becomes finding the catch.

What a good one does

Make the decision easy

The uncomfortable parts are already in it, dealt with. The reader is not defending against you — they are choosing with you.
