Read the panel, not the message

In Week 3 the errors were authorization errors and the message told you what was wrong. These are data errors. The message is usually vague or absent — but the failing module's input panel shows you exactly what arrived.

What most people do

Read the error text and guess

“Cannot read property of undefined” tells you something was missing. It does not tell you which field, which record, or why — and that is the part you need.
